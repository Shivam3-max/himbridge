About Himbridge

Himalaya + bridge: the strength and high standards of the mountains, and the trusted connection between great makers and buyers in 40+ countries.

Our mission

Himbridge is a 360° cross-border trade ecosystem. We onboard verified manufacturers from leading manufacturing clusters across selected categories and give international buyers one trusted portal to discover products, raise RFQs, compare quotations, place orders and track goods door to door.

Himbridge sits in the middle as the trust and operations layer: vendor verification, quality inspection, export documentation, freight booking and milestone-based payments.

Vendor verification process

  1. Online registration with company and niche details
  2. KYC documents — export registration, tax registration and business licence
  3. Certification review (ISO, BSCI, FSC, CE, GoodWeave…)
  4. Video or physical factory audit
  5. Sample evaluation for listed SKUs
  6. Verified badge issued — vendor can quote on RFQs
